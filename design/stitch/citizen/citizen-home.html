High Water Survival Protocols

Mandatory precautions in rising water areas

waves

Avoid Moving Water Entirely

Just six inches of fast-moving flood water can knock an adult off their feet. Twelve inches can carry a vehicle away. Never walk or drive through standing depth.

warning_amber

Never Enter Flooded Basements or Cellars

Rapid pressure can collapse foundation walls or create electrical live-wire hazards in standing sump water. Move to second floors or roof decks if stairs are unobstructed.

power_settings_new

Turn Off Main Electrical Breaker If Safe

Only turn off power if the breaker panel is in a completely dry area. Do not step in standing water to reach an electrical switch.

Federal Civil Defense Compliance Reference Code: #FL-802

What to Prepare While Waiting

Checklist for imminent extraction

  • flashlight_on
    Signal Devices Ready Charge mobile phones on power-saving mode. Keep flashlights, whistles, or brightly colored towels visible for boat crews.
  • dry_cleaning
    Dry Clothes & Sturdy Footwear Seal one set of dry thermals in waterproof plastic bags. Wear sturdy shoes to prevent puncture wounds from hidden debris.
  • medication
    Vital Medications & ID Package prescriptions, baby formula, government IDs, and essential contact numbers in a sealed zip container around your neck.
